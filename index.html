01 // ABOUT

Who I Am

Ever since I was young, I've been fascinated by how hackers operate — the way they think, the tools they use, and the problems they solve. That curiosity never went away; it grew into a passion.

I love everything about computers — from the way systems talk to each other at the network level, to digging into binaries and finding out what's really going on under the hood. Cybersecurity is where my curiosity meets purpose.

My goal is to become a professional penetration tester — someone who thinks like an attacker to protect defenders. I'm currently building my skills through hands-on labs, CTF platforms, and real-world tools like Kali Linux and Labtainer.
